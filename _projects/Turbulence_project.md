Developing computational models to study the collective dynamics of active nematic fluids and their interactions with suspended colloidal particles. The project combines continuum hydrodynamics, particle-based modeling, and numerical simulation to explore driven soft-matter systems far from equilibrium.

The broader goal is to understand how active flows, orientational order, topological defects, and particle interactions shape emergent structures and collective behavior in active materials.
